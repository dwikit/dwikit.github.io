Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This website (dwikit.github.io) and the "Laina Home" app are personal projects of David Wikstrom.

What Laina Home is

Laina Home connects David's own Google Home account to his personal AI assistant. It signs in with Google OAuth and requests the Google Home API scope. It is used only by David and his household. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data it accesses

With David's permission, the app can read his homes, his device list and device states, and run device actions that he asks for.

How that data is used

This data is used only to show device status and carry out David's requests. It is not sold. It is not shared with third parties. It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and revoking access

Sign-in tokens are stored by the connector platform the assistant runs on. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This portfolio site is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not collect personal information through any forms.
